# TraceRoot benchmark results

18 mutation cases across 2 projects (logs, shop). Generated by `bench/run_bench.py`; a hit = right file, line within +/-1 of the mutated line.

## Accuracy and model cost

| Method | Top-1 | Top-3 | Found at all | Avg model tokens / case | Avg latency |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| TraceRoot | 15/18 (83%) | 16/18 (89%) | 17/18 (94%) | 7,441 | 8.2 s |
| Baseline: Claude + full files | 16/18 (89%) | 18/18 (100%) | 18/18 (100%) | 7,360 | 8.1 s |

TraceRoot used **1% more model tokens** per case than the full-file baseline.
